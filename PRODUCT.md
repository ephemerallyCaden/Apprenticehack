# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JS (explicitly requested by the user: separate files, no framework)

## Users

Degree apprentices who organize and take part in an internal hackathon group; visitors are prospective apprentice members and event attendees deciding whether to join or register.

## Product Purpose

A team site for a Degree Apprentice Hackathon Group. Its job is to introduce the group and drive two actions: register for the hackathon and meet the team.

## Positioning

Not a generic hackathon/event template — the group's identity is that its members are degree apprentices (working professionals studying part-time), building alongside a day job.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "ApprenticeHack" (pinned, bold display treatment).
- Palette: dark navy base with electric blue accents (pinned by brief).
- Typography: clean, modern sans-serif (pinned by brief).
- Hero must include an animated particle + gradient background (pinned by brief).
- CTAs: primary "Register Now", secondary "Meet the Team" (placeholder anchors until later sections exist).

## Evidence on Hand

No real copy, logo, or roster provided yet. Tagline is authored to a "community: apprentices building together" angle per the user's direction. Team/roster/registration details do not exist yet — later prompts will build those sections; this pass is hero-only.

## Product Principles

- Apprentice identity first: never generic "hackathon team" boilerplate.
- Built, not templated: earn the bold "ApprenticeHack" name with real motion and a real dark-navy/electric-blue system, not a default dark-mode SaaS look.
- Hero is a thesis, not a header: the particle/gradient system and the two CTAs should be legible and working within seconds.
- Scope discipline: hero section only this pass; do not build nav, footer, or other sections yet.
