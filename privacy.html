Privacy policy

Last updated 25 August 2026.

ApprenticeHack is the data controller for the information collected through the registration form on this site: your name, email, company or university, apprentice/student status, experience level, and any interests you tick.

We use this only to place you in a hackathon team and email you about the event, and we keep it only as long as needed for that. This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers.

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your data at any time by emailing privacy@apprenticehack.co.uk, and you can complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk if you think we've mishandled it.
