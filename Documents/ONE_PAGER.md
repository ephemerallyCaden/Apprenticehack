## The Problem

Apprentices are missing out big time in the tech field. Uni students get hackathons handed to them; societies, build weekends, and the freedom to experiment with cutting-edge tech. Apprentices are told that because their role is already "hands-on," they don't need those experiences. But daily tickets and maintenance aren't the same as the buzz of creating something truly yours, being recognised by huge companies, and overcoming the real challenges of actualising novel ideas.

The data backs the feeling:

- Degree apprentices report **massively lower satisfaction** than full-time students: course organisation 57% vs 72%, intellectual stimulation 78% vs 84% (NSS 2023).
- Meanwhile, **76% of young people value enrichment experience; only 26% get any**. Support is weakest outside full-time education (Youth Voice Census 2025).

## The Market Gap

- **31,410 digital apprenticeship starts in 2024/25 — more than double 2020/21.** Degree-level starts: 60,000+ per year and growing (DfE).
- Competition-based learning works: **91% of WorldSkills UK competitors say competing improved their technical knowledge.**
- Employers gain too: hackathons can be mapped to apprenticeship standards and evidenced as **off-the-job training hours** — turning "a day off for a hackathon" into documented programme compliance and a retention story.

## Our Mission

**Apprenticehack forms teams of apprentices and takes them to hackathons.** Every few weeks, we pick an event, handle the logistics, and show up together. No apprentice should finish their programme without ever having built something fast, ambitious, and theirs - no apprentice should have to navigate these large spaces alone.

We give apprentices what university students take for granted: the energy, the portfolio, the network, and the proof that apprentices can harness their tech skills through more avenues than just Jira tickets. **Apprentices build.**

## What's In It For You?

- **Skills you won't learn from your job** Ship something with Claude, multi-agent frameworks, or whatever's cutting-edge that weekend, at a pace that will give you invaluable skills.
- **A network beyond your company.** You can often feel stuck with a network limited by your role, colleagues 3x your age and 0.1x your energy. Meet ambitious engineers your age: the future peers, referrals and future cofounders you'll actually connect with as friends.
- **Your own team.** No isolation or walking into a 700-person venue alone. Tell us you're in, and you turn up with a team of apprentices who've got your back.
- **Portfolio gold.** Real projects, prize wins, and demos with your name on them. Everyone wants "won a national hackathon" on their CV 😁 
- **It counts...** Every event comes with skills mapping, reflection template, and a boatload of evidence so your hackathon can double as loggable OTJ (off-the-job) training.
- **The buzz.** Free pizza, free swag, sponsor prizes. This is the part of tech you were promised.

All of it with people who get what being an apprentice is like.

## Our Future Vision

- **Our Launch event** A big internal hackathon for apprentices, from 12pm-6pm, everyone technical or not is invited. Food after!
- **Our Purpose** A London-based community sending apprentice teams into the existing UK hackathon circuit. You benefit by networking with top engineers your age, building your technical skillset, AND you can even log it as off-the-job evidence.
- **Our Partnerships** Mini-hackathons hosted at employer offices, partnering with leading apprenticeship organisations - companies provide venue and food; we provide format, community, and their apprentices' most innovative weekend of the year.
- **The National Apprentice Hackathon.** A flagship event during National Apprenticeship Week, run with partners across the apprenticeship ecosystem.
