Levelling the playing field

University students get built-in access to hackathons, sponsor showcases, and the networks that turn a weekend project into a career. Degree apprentices don't get that, even though we're splitting our time between a job and a degree just as hard. Most events are still scheduled, marketed, and run like a lecture timetable is the only shape a student's life can take, and that gap isn't an accident. It won't close on its own.

So we started ApprenticeHack: an initiative built by degree apprentices, for degree apprentices. Same sponsors, same prizes, same mentors and stage, now in front of people who are already proving themselves on the job. No lecture-hall assumptions, no "students only" fine print. If you're building your career while you build your degree, this is the level playing field nobody handed you, so we built it ourselves.
